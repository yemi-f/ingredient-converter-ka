What is this?

getgrams.cc is a browser-based tool that converts volume measurements to weight in grams for specific ingredients. Unlike generic converters, it accounts for the actual density of each ingredient — a cup of flour weighs very differently than a cup of sugar.

Why it was built

I'm an avid baker who only bakes with a scale.

  • Most recipes are written in cups, but I bake by weight
  • King Arthur Baking has a great weight chart but no calculator
  • So I built one. Made in the kitchen by Yemi

Supported units

Cup Tablespoon Teaspoon Milliliter Liter Fluid Ounce Pint Quart

Data source

Ingredient weights are sourced from the King Arthur Baking ingredient weight chart, a well-established reference used by professional and home bakers.
